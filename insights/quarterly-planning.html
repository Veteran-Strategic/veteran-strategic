Quarterly planning

A planning day that is not on the calendar again is a one-off

Most quarterly planning workshops die in the notes. The team had a good conversation. Nobody owns the cut. Ninety days later they have the same argument.

A useful day ends with three things in writing: what is in for the quarter, what is explicitly out, and who owns each next step. If those three are missing, you rented a room.

The companies that get value from an outside facilitator book the next one before they leave. Four days a year is a cadence. One day is a project.
