Process

How a day works

We do not show up and improvise a strategy offsite. The useful part starts before the room and ends in writing.

Before

A short call with the person who owns the meeting. What decision has to exist by the end of the day. Who is in the room. What is already decided and should not be reopened.

In the room

One of us, or both, runs the meeting. Time, queue, and the board. Your leaders talk. They do not also have to moderate, watch the clock, and pull quiet people in.

After

A written cut: what was chosen, what was dropped, who owns the next step. If the day worked, we book the next one on the quarterly calendar.
